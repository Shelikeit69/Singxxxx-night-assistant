How I would build it

Step 1 · Answer

Reply to common questions at night

  • Knowledge base from the website product list and the FAQ, in English and Chinese
  • Covers price, unlock methods, battery, warranty, install time, door/gate fit
  • Anything unclear goes to a person the next morning
Step 2 · Collect

Turn the chat into a ready ticket

  • Ask property type, door or gate, postal code, preferred day, photo of the door
  • Postal code gives the area, so installs can be grouped by area
  • Door type gives an install-time estimate (FAQ: wooden door ~2h, metal gate ~1h)
Step 3 · Connect

Check stock and open slots

  • Read one shared inventory sheet (Google Sheets or Lark Base)
  • Later: read the installers' calendar to suggest real slots
  • Price and install date are always confirmed by a person
Questions I would need answered first
  1. Where is stock tracked today: Shopify, Excel, Lark, or by memory?
  2. Roughly how many enquiries come in after 6pm, and on which channel?
  3. Is the company WhatsApp number already on the WhatsApp Business app or API?
  4. Who picks up the night tickets in the morning, sales or operations?
  5. Which answers should never be automated (discounts, special doors, commercial sites)?
Limits and risks, stated upfront
  • Automated WhatsApp replies need the WhatsApp Business Platform through a provider, with business verification and per-conversation costs. To be assessed before committing.
  • Customer phone numbers, addresses and door photos are personal data under the PDPA. Access and storage need to be decided.
  • The bot gives guidance, not commitments. A wrong promise costs more than a slow reply.
  • The website has a few inconsistent figures (e.g. install time 60–90 min vs 1–2 h; battery 2–3 vs 3–4 months). These should be settled before the bot quotes them.
